## Conflicts and caveats
- Year Samsø became self-sufficient differs: Lex "Siden 2007 har Samsø været selvforsynende med energi" (fetched); da.wikipedia summary 2007; CSR page summary says the 100 % milestone was 2003; a search snippet of energiakademiet's archive said 1998 (page returned HTTP 500, not fetched). Do NOT state one year as fact; if needed attribute 2007 to Lex.
- Offshore turbines: Samsø Havvind A/S set up 2003; park built 2002/2003 (da.wikipedia / en.wikipedia disagree slightly); onshore park operational 2000 (en.wikipedia summary).
- Population: 3.658 (DR, recent, undated on the page) vs 3.617 (da.wikipedia summary, "januar 2026") vs 3.724 (en.wikipedia, 2017). Cite one with attribution or say "omkring 3.600-3.700".
- F4 is a share of INVESTMENT (70 % of 440 mio. kr.), not a share of turbines owned. F5 describes ownership BEFORE the 2018 sale; the sale of 9 of the 10 offshore turbines to Wind Estate A/S was announced 12 Nov 2018 (energy-supply.dk: "9 ud af 10 havvindmøller til Wind Estate A/S"), so the offshore park is no longer mainly island-owned if completed. Completion (search snippet: 2019) not confirmed on a fetched page. Onshore turbines' present ownership not checked.
- F3 (DR kommunalvalg) and F4/F8 (DR viden) are different DR articles from different years; DR viden calls Samsø 100 % CO2-neutral (DR's wording).
- da.wikipedia lists two ferry routes (Hov/Aarhus to Sælvig; Kalundborg to Ballen); Hov vs Hou spelling differs by source.
- Turbine sizes (11 x 1 MW on land, offshore 2,3 MW each, CSR page) not included as facts; one-source only.
