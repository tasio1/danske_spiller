## Conflicts and caveats
- **EU status (resolved):** the planning note "protected across the EU" is only half right. The wolf was strictly protected (habitatdirektivet bilag IV) until 2025 and is now merely "beskyttet" (bilag V). Danish text must say "beskyttet", NOT "strengt beskyttet". European Parliament approved 8.5.2025 (F8); the Danish agency expected the national rule in force 1.7.2025 (F9, "anticipates", not confirmed as done). SNIPPET-ONLY (search summary, not fetched): the change entered into force in the EU on 7 March 2025 after the Bern Convention committee agreed on 6 Dec 2024. Do not use that date without a fetch.
- **Outdated page:** lf.dk ulven-i-danmark (undated) still says "Ulven er strengt beskyttet efter EU's habitatdirektiv" - older than the 2025 change; do not cite for status.
- **49 wolves:** the planning note "7 packs x 7 = ~49" is arithmetic from F6+F7; the ulveatlas April 2026 page gives no total (one WebFetch run even returned ~70, a miscalculation, not a page figure). Other totals exist: spring 2025 ulveatlas (1.4.2025): 6 packs, 2 pairs, 1 male, "ca. 42 individer", and the old method gave 60-80 wolves by end of 2024; DN 2025: "omkring 40 ulve"; SNIPPET-ONLY: 49 adult wolves in Jutland end of April (effektivtlandbrug 122625) and a preliminary 77 wolves after 60 pups in 2026. A text must name the year and the method; 49 is a conversion estimate, not a count.
- **Year of the packs figure:** 7 packs/3 pairs/1 male is spring 2026 (monitoring year 2026/2027, page dated 18.5.2026). Spring 2025 was 6 packs/2 pairs/1 male.
- **239 attacks:** the source says "i Jylland"; netnatur says "239 dokumenterede ulveangreb". Both are 2025. Articles are dated July 2026.
- **91 vs 89:** SGAV first published 89 for 2024, corrected to 91 on 9.7.2025 (some URLs/snippets still say 89). Use 91. The 2023 value 57 is from the same SGAV sentence.
- **36,3 mio.:** a lower bound ("mindst"), covers compensation AND fence subsidies; effektivtlandbrug attributes it to TV 2's calculation, netnatur states it directly. About 35 mio. kr. was granted in 2025 to wolf-proof fences alone (effektivtlandbrug), so most of the 36,3 mio. is fence money. Do not write "36 mio. i erstatning".
- **Fences do not always protect:** DN (April 2025): one attack behind functioning wolf-proof fences in about 8 years; netnatur (July 2026): ca. 80 animals in 10 incidents behind functioning fences in 2025. Different dates; do not write "never".
- **Compensation total:** 1.239+32+5+8+1 = 1.285, consistent. Netnatur headline says "flere end 1.200", body "flere end 1.280".
- **Agriculture demand for a plan:** F11 is Agillix only. A WebFetch of L&F's page said L&F "does not demand a specific population target" and works long-term to change the wolf's international protection status; a search snippet claimed L&F wants to discuss a target if the population keeps rising (SNIPPET-ONLY, not on the fetched page). Phrase the group position as "landboforeninger", not "L&F".

## UNCONFIRMED
- What compensation does NOT cover (fence maintenance, fear, time): not confirmed. A 2020 LandbrugsAvisen article says Miljøstyrelsen rejected a maintenance application because "vedligehold ikke er tilskudsberettiget", but a search snippet says later rules cover maintenance for five years. Current status unresolved; leave out. Fear/psychological burden: no source found.
- Danish law since 1998 requires outdoor livestock keepers to protect animals "bedst muligt" with no legal right to compensation (DN ulv page, summarised only): not verified verbatim.
