DanskBlogSJOVT DANSK

Learn Danish grammar with free games

Sjovt Dansk (“Fun Danish”) is a free collection of short browser games for adults learning Danish, from A1 to B2. Read a simple guide in English, then practise in a game. The games themselves are in Danish, which is part of the training. No login, no ads, nothing to install.
