## Conflicts and caveats
- Several "national" figures exist for different scopes and years; never mix them: 881.062 ton (all sectors, Miljøstyrelsen via Stop Spild Af Mad; a search snippet links it to 2022, the fetched sentence has no year); household series 261.000 ton (2011/2012) to 247.000 ton (2017) on the same page (14.000 ton difference matches F5); and Miljøstyrelsen's household "mad" 507.000-610.000 ton (mst.dk, 12 May 2023, includes inedible parts, so not the same as madspild).
- en.wikipedia (Stop Wasting Food) says Denmark wastes "over 700,000 tons" (Danish Agriculture and Food Council): an older estimate, not comparable to 881.062.
- F3 (25 %, 2010-2015) is a Wikipedia claim; no Miljøstyrelsen time series was fetched. Attribute it, do not credit it all to Stop Spild Af Mad.
- F7/F8: Too Good To Go figures are the company's claims as of August 2023; the 2015 Danish origin is secure.
- F11 is a changed interpretation by Fødevarestyrelsen, not a new law; the date comes only from the URL (2025/dec).
- Founding: 31 July 2008 (FødevareFOKUS) agrees with "2008" (Wikipedia).

## UNCONFIRMED
