## Conflicts and caveats
- DATE OF FIRST FEST IN REBILD: rebildfesten.dk says "The following year, on July 4, 1912, the first Rebild Celebration took place" (FETCHED); da.wikipedia says "Den første Rebildfest fandt sted 5. august 1912" and en.wikipedia says August 5, 1912 (after King Frederik VIII's death). The booklet should say only "i 1912", never a day.
- "Since 1912" vs F8 "hvert år siden 1914": do not write "hvert år siden 1912".
- LAND SIZE: 56 ha is NOT confirmed. Found: 80 ha (Dansk Biografisk Leksikon, F6); about 200 acres / 0.81 km2 (en.wikipedia); 140 tønder (da.wikipedia, F4; conversion not sourced); a search summary attributed "56 hectares" to an unnamed Danish source I never opened. The plan's "56 ha" is UNCONFIRMED; do not use. Safest: "ca. 80 ha" citing Biografisk Leksikon, or leave size out.
- Who bought and when differs (da.wikipedia: Henius bought 1910; Biografisk Leksikon: a committee under Henius; en.wikipedia: bought 1911 with Danish-American funds). Say "dansk-amerikanere under ledelse af Max Henius".
- 1909 in Aarhus was a precursor tied to Landsudstillingen (F3), not the first Rebild fest. guide-usa.dk says "Lige siden 1909 er USA's Independence Day blevet fejret i Danmark."
- Nixon rests on one source (en.wikipedia Rebild Festival). Disney and Reagan have two (F9, F10).
- Queen Margrethe II as protektor (da.wikipedia) may be outdated after her 2024 abdication; not used.
- Programme and ticket facts on guide-usa.dk are from 2022 and not current.
