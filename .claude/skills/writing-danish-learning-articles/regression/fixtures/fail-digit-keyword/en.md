# Danish language test 3: how the exam is built

Danish language test 3 has three parts. You read, you write, and you speak. Each part gets its
own grade. Here is a short overview, so you know what you are walking into.

## The parts of the exam

Reading comes first. Then you write a text. Last, you speak with an examiner. You sit all three
parts within the same exam term.

## What to do now

Find your provider. Ask about the dates for the term you are aiming at. Put the deadline in your
calendar before you start revising.
