## Conflicts and caveats
- Visitors per year: lex.dk "anslået til 1,8 millioner" in 2025; en.wikipedia "2.5–2.9 million visitors per year" (undated); search snippets (not fetched) mention 2,2 million, "up to two million" and 1.75 million. Use only the lex.dk figure, with "2025" and "anslået".
- Rides: bakken.dk "36 forlystelser" (undated); da.wikipedia "32 kørende forlystelser (2013)"; en.wikipedia infobox "Total 33". Use "36 ifølge Bakkens egen hjemmeside" or no number.
- Name: bakken.dk uses the old spelling "Kirstine Piil" ("Kirstine Piil var den, som fandt det Kilde-Vand, 1583."); other sources "Kirsten Piil". 1583 is "ifølge traditionen" (legend), not an archival date; write "ifølge sagnet".
- F6: 1756 is free access to the Dyrehave; no fetched source says "Bakken has had free entry since 1756" in so many words.
- en.wikipedia (fetch extract) also says the area got the name Jægersborg Dyrehave in 1671; bakken.dk gives Christian V as the one who expanded the park after becoming king in 1670 (the latter from a search snippet). Do not state a naming year.
