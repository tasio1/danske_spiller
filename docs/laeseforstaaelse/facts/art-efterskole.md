## Conflicts and caveats
- **Year matters:** the freshest data is 2026/27 (F1-F3, published 27.8.2026); 2025/26 data (F4-F5) was published 29.8.2025. A text should use one year consistently or name both.
- **10th-grade count for 2025/26:** the 2025 release says 24.408; the 2026 release says 24.212 "sidste år". Same school year, two numbers (probably revised or differently counted). Use 2026/27 (24.804) and avoid quoting the 2025/26 10th-grade count.
- **Total drop:** 2026 total 30.911 is 0.3 % / 107 fewer than 31.018 (search snippet of the 2026 release; arithmetic matches F5). Pupils in 9th grade fell from 5.763 to 5.159 (2026 release).
- **Share of an age cohort:** "36,2 procent" is the share of the cohort in 10th grade only; 9th-grade share in 2025/26 was 8,4 procent (2025 release). No fetched source gives one all-in share of a cohort. A skagenonline snippet says "12 procent" (SNIPPET-ONLY, secondary, unclear basis): do not use.
- **Number of schools:** 238 (Aug 2025) vs "alle 234 efterskoler" answering the survey in Aug 2026. Close but not the same year; 234 is presumably the 2026 count, but the page does not say "there are 234 efterskoler" explicitly. Prefer 238 with year, or "godt 230".
- **Price:** efterskolerne.dk says schools set their own weekly price. Search snippets (SNIPPET-ONLY, school sites) give ca. 2.600-3.000 kr. per week, 110.000-125.000 kr. per year before support; do not use without a fetch of a source. F10: the 58.800 kr. example assumes an income given on the page (a snippet says 400.000 kr.); the same page shows 82.278 kr. if the parents' income doubles.
- **Funding facts F11/F12** carry year 2023 and are from VIFO (a government-funded information site), not UVM itself.
- 14 years or 7th grade completed is the entry rule on borger.dk Life in Denmark; the sheet's F6 uses "14-18 år" from VIFO (2024).
