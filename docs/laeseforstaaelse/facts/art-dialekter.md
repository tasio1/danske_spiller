## Conflicts and caveats
- **Age of sources:** F1-F6 are from 2015, F10-F12 from 2019, F7-F8 from 2024. Write "i en undersøgelse fra 2015", never "i dag". No newer figure on how many young Bornholmers speak bornholmsk was found.
- **No reason given:** the DR 2015 article explicitly does not know why Bornholm differs (F3); isolation is mentioned only as a fact about the area, not as a proven cause. The text may say "forskerne ved det ikke" but must NOT claim a cause. The assignment's "why bornholmsk dies" has no sourced answer beyond F3 and F6 (general standardisation).
- **F5 phrasing:** the sentence is a researcher's statement in a DR article about the country as a whole ("land"), not a peer-reviewed ranking. The text must not name the researcher or quote her; state it as "ifølge en KU-forsker i 2015" or leave out the attribution.
- **Institution names:** the 2015 DR piece names Nordisk Forskningsinstitut (KU) for the standardisation article and "Sprogforandringscentret og Center for Dialektforskning ved Københavns Universitet" for the Bornholm article; the 2024 KU page names Center for Dialektforskning at "Institut for Nordiske Studier og Sprogvidenskab". Institute names have changed over time; use "Københavns Universitet" plus the two centre names.
- **AI project:** Videnskab.dk 2019 calls it a precursor of a translation tool; no 2025-26 status was found, so do not say it is finished. It is led by KU adjunct Alex Speed Kjeldsen with Leon Derczynski (ITU); names are real people, not to be quoted in the game text.
- Search snippet (SNIPPET-ONLY): Sprogforandringscentret had a project "Dialekt i periferien: Bornholm, Vendsyssel og Sønderjylland"; the research page confirmed only the title, not a description.

## UNCONFIRMED
- That the "melody" (tonefald) of bornholmsk is gone among the young (planning note): the fetched DR article does not mention melody. Leave out.
- That "no Bornholm dialect features" remain in young speakers' language: only the weaker "stort set ikke længere dialekt" (F1) is sourced.
- Why sønderjysk and vendelbomål survive: no cause stated in any fetched source (the KU 2024 page says only that young sønderjyder still speak dialect, with changes towards standard Danish).
- Number of bornholmsk speakers today; the 2019 quote that there will be no more informants "om 10 til 20 år" (fetched, quote below) has not been re-verified and is time-bound: "Der om 10 til 20 år ikke er flere informanter tilbage, som vi kan trække på ved kortlægningen af traditionelt bornholmsk." (videnskab.dk, 2019) - usable only as "forskere vurderede i 2019".
