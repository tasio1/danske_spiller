## Conflicts and caveats
- COUNT CONFLICT (19.773 / 62.000 / ~40.000 / 60.000). The figures count different things or come from different years and sources. (a) 19.773 = Boligforeningsweb analysis of Danmarks Statistik numbers for 2024; the page says nearly half lie in København, Ballerup and Tårnby and 25 kommuner have none. It defines kolonihaver only as "små jordstykker lejet ud til private", so it is probably a registry-based count that covers far fewer than all gardens. I could not find the method; WHY it is about a third of 62.000 is UNCONFIRMED. (b) ca. 62.000 = lex.dk, all gardens, in godt 1.000 foreninger (no year shown). (c) ca. 60.000 = Trap Danmark, 2013, and Wikipedia "i dag". (d) ca. 40.000 = only gardens that are members of Kolonihaveforbundet (about 400 foreninger per a search summary, SNIPPET-ONLY). Rule for the coder: never write one number as "the" total; name source and year, or avoid a total. Safest statements: "ca. 62.000 haver i godt 1.000 foreninger" and "ca. 40.000 er medlem af Kolonihaveforbundet".
- The plan's "~40.000 plots in 400 associations" is half-supported: 40.000 is FETCHED (F4); 400 associations is SNIPPET-ONLY.
- A summary of the DR article (dr.dk, 6 Dec 2024) said "60.000 kolonihaver associations", almost certainly garbled; do not use that article's numbers.
- Rules differ by kommune. Copenhagen (F11): overnatning 1 April-31 Oct. Aalborg Kommune (bestemmelser of 22 Dec 2022, fetched): no overnatning 1 Nov-31 Mar, hovedhus højst 50 m2, ekstra bygninger i alt 30 m2. kolonihave.nu says winter half is 1 Oct-31 Mar. Never present one m2 limit or one season as national law.
- Wikipedia (F7) gives 20.000 in 1904; lex.dk (paraphrase) gives about 20.000 in 1907. Use only one, with its source.
